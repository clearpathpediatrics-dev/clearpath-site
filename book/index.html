What we'll cover

  • Where things stand. The doctors, appointments and paperwork involved right now.

Questions

Is the call really free?

Yes. It is a free 30-minute call with no cost and no commitment, and no one will pressure you to sign up for anything.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. Come as you are. If it is easy to have your child's list of doctors, any recent letters such as a denial or a referral, and your top questions nearby, the half hour goes further, but none of it is required.

Is this medical advice?

No. ClearPath Pediatrics provides care navigation and education, never medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Medical decisions stay with your child's licensed providers. If your child has a medical emergency, call 911.
