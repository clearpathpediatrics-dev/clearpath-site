Common questions

Does this tell me what to give my child?
No. It formats what your prescriber has already ordered. Every dose and time comes from you, and we never review or change it.

Is my child's medication list stored anywhere?
No. It runs entirely in your browser and nothing is transmitted.

Why print two copies?
One for the fridge, one for the care binder. The binder copy is what an ER or a new specialist needs.

How often should I update it?
Whenever a prescriber changes something, plus a full check against the chart twice a year. Lists drift when several teams prescribe.
