Pediatric Discharge Medications Not Ready at the Pharmacy? A Parent's Action Plan

August 31, 2026 · 4 min read · ClearPath Pediatrics

You've packed up the hospital room, buckled your child into the car seat, and pulled into the pharmacy drive-thru — only to hear, "We don't have that ready yet." After a stressful stay, that pause can feel like the last thing you have energy for. Take a breath. Discharge medication hiccups are common, and most of them are fixable with a few organized phone calls. This guide walks you through what to check and who to call, without giving any medical advice.
